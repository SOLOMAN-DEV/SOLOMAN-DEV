1 · What the CRM does

The Sabar Mart CRM is the one place that keeps track of everything happening around the marketplace: who your customers are and what they buy, how each vendor is performing and what they are owed, which affiliates bring in sales, and which problems need someone's attention.

What it handles for you, automatically

AreaExamples
CustomersTracks each customer from signup to loyal and VIP, and notices abandoned carts. Awards loyalty points with reward milestones, routes support tickets to the right team, and recommends products (only to customers who agreed to marketing).
VendorsDocument checks and store setup steps, plus scorecards for fulfilment, late shipping, returns and reviews. A full money ledger: every sale, the commission, GST on commission, TCS and TDS, refunds and payouts.
AffiliatesValidates referral clicks, credits orders to the right affiliate, and pays tiered commission only after the 14-day return window. Takes commission back if a paid order is refunded later.
TeamsRaises alerts as tasks, for example a vendor below 3.5★ or a refund over ₹10,000. Gives each person their own key and logs who did what.
The screen you'll use today Right now staff work in the CRM's built-in API screen, /docs, shown throughout this guide. It's a little technical, but every button does exactly one job and each person only sees what their role allows. A friendlier dashboard can be built later on top of the same system.

2 · Getting started

Step 1: open the CRM screen

Open your CRM address followed by /docs in Chrome, Edge or Firefox, for example https://crm.sabarmart.com/docs. You'll see the list of everything the CRM can do, grouped by area.

Step 2: log in with your key

  1. Click the green Authorize button at the top right.
  2. Paste your personal key into Value. Your administrator gives you this key once; it starts with smc_.
  3. Click Authorize, then Close. The padlocks now show as closed.
Keep your key private Your key is your identity. Everything done with it is recorded in the audit log under your name. Never share it, never put it in chat or email, and tell an admin straight away if you think someone else has it. They can switch it off in seconds.

Step 3: check who you are logged in as

Open GET /me, click Try it out, then Execute. The answer shows your name, your role and everything you're allowed to do.

How every action works: the same five clicks

  1. Click the bar of the action you need, for example Customer Profile, to open it.
  2. Click Try it out on the right.
  3. Fill in the boxes. Under Parameters that means IDs such as C-1. Where there's a Request body, edit the example text.
  4. Click the big blue Execute button.
  5. Read the answer under Server response: a code plus the response body.

What the answer codes mean

CodeMeaningWhat to do
200 / 201 / 204DoneNothing; it worked. 201 means something new was created. 204 means done, with nothing to show.
401Not logged inClick Authorize and paste your key again. Your key may have been replaced.
403Not allowed for your roleThis is on purpose (see Figure 5). Ask the right team, or an admin if you think you need access.
404Not foundCheck the ID (customer, order, vendor) for typos.
409A business rule said noRead the detail message, e.g. "return window of 14 days has closed".
422Something in the form is wrongA box is missing or in the wrong format, e.g. a negative amount.
500Unexpected errorSend the error_id to your admin. An alert has already gone to them automatically.

4 · Support team

Your ticket queue

Open support › GET /tickets, then Try it out › Execute. Tickets are already sorted, most urgent first. The CRM reads each message and sets its priority, its team and the reasons for that choice.

The CRM sees…It does…
fraud, hacked, unauthorized, legal, policeCritical; goes to trust & safety, and an urgent task is raised automatically
payment, charged, card, UPIGoes to the payments team
refund, return, exchangeGoes to returns & refunds
delivery, courier, late, missingGoes to logistics
angry words, a high-value order, a loyal or VIP customerPriority is raised; VIP general questions go to the VIP concierge

Look up a customer

Open customers › GET /customers/{customer_id} and enter the customer's ID. You'll see their stage (e.g. loyal), lifetime value, loyalty points, marketing consent and open tickets (see Figure 6a).

Give a customer a refund

Open refunds › POST /orders/{order_id}/refunds, enter the order ID, and in the request body type the amount and a short reason.

Refund rules to know
  • Over ₹10,000: the refund waits for finance to approve it. You can't approve your own request.
  • Delivered orders only: an order that hasn't been delivered yet should be cancelled instead.
  • No more than was paid: you can never refund more than the customer paid, and refunds still waiting for approval count against that amount.

A customer asks "what data do you hold about me?"

Open privacy › GET /customers/{customer_id}/export. The answer contains everything held about them: profile, consent, orders, refunds, tickets and browsing. Click Download and send them the file through your normal secure channel. The export is recorded in the audit log.

5 · Finance team

Approve or reject large refunds

Every refund over ₹10,000 appears as a pending_review refund and as a task in the finance queue.

  1. Open refunds › GET /refunds, type pending_review in status, and Execute to see what's waiting.
  2. Check the order and the reason. If it's fine, open POST /refunds/{refund_id}/approve, enter the refund ID and Execute. If not, use /reject instead.

Where the money from a sale goes

Sabar Mart prices include GST. When an order is delivered, the CRM records the sale and everything withheld from it. Here is a real example: a ₹1,180 kurta with 18% GST.

Vendor balance and payouts

Open finance › GET /vendors/{vendor_id}/ledger. payable_now is what you can pay today. Money from orders still inside the 14-day return window is held back (held_in_return_window), because the customer could still return them.

To pay a vendor, use POST /vendors/{vendor_id}/payouts. It pays payable_now, at most once every 7 days, and only to verified vendors.

Tax report for filing

Open finance › GET /finance/tax-report and give a start and end date, e.g. the first day of this month and of next month. For each vendor it shows sales including GST, the taxable value, TCS collected, TDS deducted, and commission with its GST. Use these figures for GSTR-8 and the 194-O return.

Confirm the rates with your Chartered Accountant The default rates are TCS 0.5%, TDS 0.1% (5% without a PAN) and 18% GST on commission. They follow the law as it stood in late 2024, and they live in one settings file (config.py). Have your CA confirm them before go-live, and again after any Budget change. Threshold exemptions are not modelled.

Affiliate payouts

Open GET /affiliates/{affiliate_id}/payout-preview to see what a payout would pay right now. If it's right, run POST /affiliates/{affiliate_id}/payouts.

  • Paid once: each order's commission is paid only once. Running a payout again pays only what's new.
  • Clawbacks: if a paid order is refunded later, the next payout automatically deducts the difference.
  • Tiers: Bronze 3%, Silver 5% (25+ completed orders), Gold 7% (100+) and Platinum 10% (500+). An order's rate is fixed when it's first paid.

6 · Vendor team

Onboarding a new vendor

  1. POST /vendors: create the vendor with an ID, store name and contact email.
  2. PUT /vendors/{id}/documents/{doc}: record each document when it's received. The documents are business_registration, tax_id, pan_card, bank_details and identity_proof.
  3. POST …/documents/{doc}/review: approve or reject each one, with {"approved": true} or {"approved": false}.
  4. PUT /vendors/{id}/milestones/{m}: tick off each store setup step: profile_completed, first_product_listed, shipping_configured and payout_account_linked.
  5. GET /vendors/{id} shows the progress, what's still missing (next_actions) and whether the vendor can go live.

Your alerts: the vendor_success queue

Open internal › GET /tasks with team vendor_success. The CRM raises these tasks by itself. The same problem never creates a duplicate task; the existing task is updated instead. When you've dealt with one, close it with POST /tasks/{task_id}/resolve.

AlertWhenPriority
Low review scoreAverage review below 3.5★High
Low fulfilmentFewer than 95% of orders fulfilled (too many cancellations)High
Shipping delaysMore than 10% of shipments lateMedium
High returnsMore than 15% of delivered orders returnedMedium

While a vendor is flagged, their products are left out of customer recommendations.

7 · Admin and privacy

Give a new team member access

Open admin › POST /users and enter a username and a role. The answer contains their API key. It's shown only this once. Pass it to the person securely; the CRM keeps only a scrambled copy.

When…Do this
Someone lost their key, or it may be exposedPOST /users/{name}/rotate-key. The old key stops working at once.
Someone leaves the companyPOST /users/{name}/deactivate, the same day
You want to see who has accessGET /users

Audit log: who did what

GET /audit lists the latest actions. Filter by person with actor. It records every change, every refused attempt, and every look at sensitive data such as customer details, ledgers, payouts and tax reports.

Business overview

GET /analytics gives the whole picture: customers by stage, abandoned carts and marketing opt-ins; orders by status; revenue net of refunds; commission and taxes withheld; flagged vendors; affiliate sales; open tickets; and open tasks.

Privacy requests (DPDP Act 2023)

RequestHowWho
"Stop sending me offers"POST /customers/{id}/consent with {"marketing": false, "source": "phone_request"}. The store's unsubscribe link does the same automatically.Support
"What do you hold about me?"GET /customers/{id}/export (Figure 9)Support
"Delete my data"POST /customers/{id}/erase. The name, email, phone, browsing, ticket text and refund reasons are wiped. Orders and the money ledger are kept without the name, because tax law requires those records.Admin only
Erasure can't be undone The CRM refuses to erase someone who still has orders on the way or refunds waiting for a decision, or an affiliate who is still owed commission. Finish those first. After an erasure the store can't add new activity for that customer. Older backups still contain the data until they are deleted on schedule.

Outside the CRM you also need a published privacy notice, a grievance officer, and to answer requests within the timelines you promise.

8 · Connecting the Node.js store

For the store developer.

The store sends the CRM a short message, an event, every time something happens: a signup, an order, a delivery and so on. The package in sabar-mart-crm/clients/node does the hard parts. If the CRM is down, checkout keeps working and nothing is lost, and nothing is ever counted twice.

Setup, in three steps

  1. Install the package and mysql2, and create the outbox table in the store's database from clients/node/sql/outbox.mysql.sql.
  2. Ask the CRM admin for a key with the system role.
  3. Start the worker once when the app boots, and add events inside your existing database transactions:
const { CrmClient, MysqlOutboxStore, OutboxWorker, events } = require('@sabarmart/crm-client');

const crm    = new CrmClient({ baseUrl: process.env.CRM_URL, apiKey: process.env.CRM_API_KEY });
const outbox = new MysqlOutboxStore(pool);                  // your mysql2/promise pool
new OutboxWorker({ client: crm, store: outbox }).start();   // safe in every Node process

// inside the checkout transaction (conn = that transaction's connection):
await conn.query('INSERT INTO order_lines ...', [...]);
await outbox.enqueue([events.orderPlaced({
  orderId: 'L-1001', customerId: 'C-1', productId: 'P-1', amount: '1180.00',  // GST included
})], conn);
Send this event……when
customerRegisteredAn account is created. Set marketingConsent: true only if they ticked the opt-in box.
productUpsertedA product is added or changed. Always include gstRate, e.g. "0.18".
orderPlaced / orderShipped / orderDelivered / orderReturned / orderCancelledFor each order line, as it moves through delivery
customerBrowsed, cartItemAdded, cartItemRemovedShopping activity
referralClickedA product page is opened with ?ref=CODE
vendorReviewed, ticketCreated, customerConsentUpdatedReviews, support messages, marketing opt-in or opt-out

A full Express example with every hook point is in clients/node/examples/express-integration.js. Vendors must already exist in the CRM before their products arrive. The vendor team creates them (section 6).

9 · Putting it live on MilesWeb

The CRM runs on MilesWeb cPanel hosting using Setup Python App and a MySQL database. The full step-by-step guide, with exact commands, is in sabar-mart-crm/DEPLOY_MILESWEB.md. Here is the overview.

The settings (environment variables)

NameWhat to put
DATABASE_URLmysql+pymysql://USER:PASSWORD@localhost/DBNAME?charset=utf8mb4. URL-encode special characters in the password.
SABAR_CRM_API_KEYSThe store's key: LONGRANDOMKEY:system:storefront. People get personal keys with user-add instead.
SABAR_CRM_ALERT_EMAIL + SABAR_CRM_SMTP_*Who gets error alerts, and a cPanel mailbox to send them from
SABAR_CRM_DOCSLeave unset (on) while staff use the /docs screen. The page itself shows no data without a key.

Create the first admin and the team

python -m sabar_mart_crm.db user-add yourname admin     # prints your key, shown once
python -m sabar_mart_crm.db user-add priya finance
python -m sabar_mart_crm.db user-add rahul support_agent

10 · Backups, alerts and troubleshooting

Nightly backups

In cPanel › Cron Jobs add two daily jobs. Settings are read from a private file, ~/.sabar-crm.env (chmod 600); see DEPLOY_MILESWEB.md step 7.

WhenCommand
Daily 02:30/home/cpuser/sabar-mart-crm/scripts/cron.sh backup
Daily 03:15/home/cpuser/sabar-mart-crm/scripts/cron.sh purge
  • What a backup is: a single compressed file holding the whole CRM, readable only by your account. It's checked after it's written. The newest 14 are kept.
  • If one fails: an alert email is sent.
  • Copy them off the server every week. A backup on the same server doesn't help if the account is lost.
  • To restore into an empty database: python -m sabar_mart_crm.db restore FILE.

Alerts and health checks

  • Crashes: if something crashes, the admin gets an email with an error id. The person who hit the error sees the same id, so you can match them up. The same problem alerts at most once every 15 minutes.
  • Uptime monitor: point a free monitor such as UptimeRobot at https://crm.sabarmart.com/health?deep=true. It answers 200 when everything is up, and 503 when the database can't be reached.
  • Store sync: the store developer should watch outbox.stats(). pending should stay near 0, and dead should be 0.

Troubleshooting

ProblemLikely cause and fix
401 on every actionNot logged in, or your key was replaced or switched off. Click Authorize again, or ask an admin for a new key.
403 on an action you needYour role doesn't include it (section 3). Ask the right team, or ask an admin to review your role.
409 "return window … has closed"Returns are only allowed within 14 days of delivery. Use a goodwill refund instead.
409 "exceeds refundable"The amount is more than what's left to refund, including refunds still waiting for approval.
409 "customer … has been erased"That customer was deleted on request (DPDP). No new activity can be added.
503 "timed out waiting for the CRM database lock"The server is overloaded or a request got stuck. Try again in a minute. If it keeps happening, the admin will have an alert.
A new order doesn't appear in the CRMCheck the store's outbox: is the event pending (the CRM was unreachable) or dead (rejected: read last_error)? A common cause is that the vendor or product doesn't exist in the CRM yet.
The app won't start on MilesWebRead stderr.log in the app folder. Check DATABASE_URL, run Run Pip Install again, and check the Python version is 3.10 or newer.
